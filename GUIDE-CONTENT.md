# Guide Content Contract

Guides answer a concrete buying or routine-placement question. Generic background articles are not eligible.

## Eligibility

- The guide must name the decision a reader needs to make.
- It must have a justified internal-link path to relevant Review, Compare, Best, or category pages.
- Existing guides and commercial pages must be checked for overlap.
- Claims must remain within the supplied evidence boundary.

## Required brief fields

- buyer decision and target reader
- must-answer questions
- category/product roles and tradeoffs
- internal-link plan
- source URLs and Claim/Evidence map
- no-buy or simpler-alternative path
- `releaseDecision`

## Required page decisions

- how to choose between relevant formats or approaches
- when each path fits
- when not to add a product
- next-step links that follow the reader's decision rather than a generic CTA list

Research approval, content-write approval, and release approval remain separate gates.
