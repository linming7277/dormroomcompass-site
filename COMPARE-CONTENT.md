# Compare Content Contract

Compare pages must resolve one shared purchase task.

## Eligibility

- Products must be realistic alternatives for the same buyer decision.
- A two-product comparison needs at least three evidence-backed decision dimensions.
- A three-product comparison must keep all three choices; it must not be forced into an A/B template.
- A comparison cannot use missing evidence to declare a universal winner.

## Required brief fields

- compared product identities and variants
- shared purchase task
- decision dimensions and evidence per dimension
- source URLs and Claim/Evidence map
- overlap/cannibalization check
- commercial CTA strategy
- `releaseDecision`

## Required page decisions

- Choose A if…
- Choose B if…
- Choose neither if…
- For three or more products, one conditional path per product plus choose none.

Research approval, content-write approval, and release approval remain separate gates.
