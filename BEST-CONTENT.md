# Best Content Contract

Best pages are shortlist tools, not page-count targets.

## Eligibility

- A market-level Best requires at least three meaningful product families and two independent brands.
- A single-brand Best is allowed only when the title, scope, and search intent explicitly name that brand.
- Product identity, usable image, affiliate URL, media permission, and evidence boundaries must be complete before drafting.
- Existing page overlap must be checked before `createPage=true`.

## Required brief fields

- buying task and target reader
- eligible product families and excluded duplicates
- brand-diversity evidence
- selection logic and comparison dimensions
- best-for, tradeoff, and skip-if path for every pick
- Claim/Evidence map and source URLs
- commercial CTA strategy
- `releaseDecision`

## Required page decisions

- comparison table
- top/lead pick only when evidence supports one
- best for, tradeoff, skip if, and merchant CTA for each pick
- selection methodology and a clear no-buy path

Research approval, content-write approval, and release approval remain separate gates.
