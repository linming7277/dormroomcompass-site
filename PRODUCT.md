# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Careful shoppers arriving from search with a concrete product question. They want to narrow a category, compare credible alternatives, understand tradeoffs, and leave for a merchant only after they know which option fits their use case.

## Product Purpose

Provide a reusable, configuration-driven affiliate publishing system for focused product-research sites. The public experience should turn verified product-library and merchant evidence into category hubs, best-of guides, reviews, comparisons, and buying guides without inventing hands-on experience, ratings, prices, stock, or discounts.

## Brand Personality

Methodical, candid, practical. The site should feel like a well-maintained buyer's field guide: confident enough to make a recommendation, disciplined enough to show uncertainty, and efficient enough for a shopper to scan quickly.

## Anti-references

- Thin affiliate listicles that repeat merchant descriptions and hide their methodology.
- Generic AI landing pages with oversized marketing heroes, decorative cards, invented statistics, or vague trust claims.
- Pixel copies of TechGearLab, RTINGS, Wirecutter, or any competitor's branding, copy, photography, testing claims, or proprietary scoring system.
- Review pages that imply a product was purchased or tested when the evidence only supports source-based research.
- Multi-category portals with empty taxonomies, fake search controls, or routes created only to increase page count.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with the buyer's decision, not the publisher's marketing story.
2. Make comparison faster through consistent fields, awards, evidence labels, and visible tradeoffs.
3. Keep claims proportional to evidence; uncertainty is useful information.
4. Use dense but calm information architecture so shoppers can scan without losing context.
5. Let one configuration contract drive identity, taxonomy, evidence, monetization, and publishing state.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Maintain keyboard-accessible navigation, visible focus states, semantic tables, sufficient color contrast, meaningful alt text, reduced-motion-safe behavior, and layouts that remain usable at 320px without horizontal page overflow.
